**Lusted** is a Sri Lankan boutique for the hours after the lights go down. Lace that was never meant to stay on. Swim that's really about the walk from the pool to the room. Pieces for midnight that don't pretend to be anything else.

Three collections, one mood:

- **Lace Secrets** — bras, briefs, bodysuits and slips in lace so fine it's barely there, and satin that slides off at the first touch.
- **Sun Kissed** — bikinis, one-pieces and cover-ups that show exactly as much as you decide, and come off faster than they go on.
- **Midnight Sins** — the after-dark pieces: garters, teddies, cheongsams and gowns with a slit for the evenings that end the way they should.

Every piece is photographed on a model, cropped close and signed with the wordmark. The bag turns into one WhatsApp message to the shop — there's no checkout to cool you off. Sizes XS to XXL, with a size guide that tells you how each piece fits and which way to err.

The shop is built with Next.js, served from Vercel, and the photos live in a private bucket. The people who work on it keep the long version in [the handbook](docs/HANDBOOK.md).
